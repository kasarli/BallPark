# Ballpark

A number-guessing game about the size of things. How tall is Mount Everest? How many bones are in your body? How many neurons does a fruit fly have? You get six guesses to land within 10% of the real answer.

# **Play it here:** `https://kasarli.github.io/BallPark/`

## How to play

1. Pick a level from the catalog, or play the daily question.
2. Type a number and press Enter.
3. Every wrong guess tells you whether the real answer is higher or lower, and how close you are:
   - 🟩 within 2× of the answer
   - 🟨 within 10×
   - 🟥 more than 10× away
4. A guess within **10%** of the answer wins the question.
5. After 3 guesses you get a hint about how many digits the answer has. After 5 you also learn the first digit.

You can type big numbers the short way: `5k`, `2.5m`, `3b`, `1t`, or in scientific form like `6e24`. Very large answers (a quadrillion and up) are displayed like 8.1 × 10⁶⁷.

## Levels and stars

Each level has 3 questions. A level's questions stay hidden as `???` until you reach them, so you can't peek at what's coming.

| Difficulty | Levels | Stars per question | What to expect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | 5 | 1 | Small numbers you can reason your way to |
| Medium | 12 | 2 | Trivia worth knowing |
| Hard | 5 | 3 | Bigger numbers and more niche facts |
| Impossible | 4 | 5 | Huge, obscure and mostly unguessable |
| Abyss | 8 | 20 | Insanely random and niche |

**Abyss is blind.** There are no digit hints and no warmth colors or closeness bars, only "higher" or "lower".

There are 34 levels and 102 questions in total, plus locked "Coming soon" tiles for levels that haven't been written yet. Levels can be replayed from their results screen.

## Daily question

A new question appears every day, the same for everyone. It's picked from the Easy, Medium and Hard levels. Solving it keeps your 🔥 streak going, and there's a countdown to the next one.

## Sharing

Finished a level or the daily question? Press **Copy result** to get an emoji summary you can paste anywhere, for example:

```
Ballpark Oct 9 3/6
🟥⬆️
🟨⬇️
🎯
```

## Saving progress

Your progress, stars and streak are saved in your browser with `localStorage`, so they survive closing the page. They live on that one browser and device. Clearing your site data, using a private window, or switching browsers or devices starts you fresh.
